# Required evidence

## Introduction and related work

- Names at least two existing benchmarks and states a specific shortcoming of each.
- Compares existing benchmarks in a table.

## Construction

- Reports data sources and their licences.
- Describes annotation protocol and reports inter-annotator agreement, or explains why
  there was no annotation.
- Reports a contamination or leakage check.
- Has a dataset statistics table.

## Protocol and results

- Justifies each metric and names what it misses.
- States how baselines were tuned and with what budget.
- Reports variance across runs or seeds for every number in the main table.
- Includes a per-category breakdown.

## Analysis

- Has an error taxonomy with at least one example per category.
- States where all methods fail.

## Whole paper

- Data, code and evaluation harness are released with a URL and licence.
- Has a limitations section that says what the benchmark cannot measure.
