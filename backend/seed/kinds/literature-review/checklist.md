# Required evidence

## Introduction

- Research questions stated explicitly and numbered.
- Explains why the review is needed now.
- Names prior reviews and says how this one differs, or states there are none.

## Method

- Names every database or source searched.
- Gives the exact query strings.
- Lists inclusion and exclusion criteria.
- Reports the count of papers at each screening stage, ideally as a funnel figure.
- States how many reviewers screened and how disagreements were resolved.
- Describes what was extracted from each paper.

## Results

- Each research question has its own subsection that answers it.
- Counts are given for each category, with a table or chart.
- Every category cites at least one representative paper.

## Discussion

- Gaps are stated as specific missing combinations, not "more work is needed".
- Implications are split for researchers and for practitioners.

## Whole paper

- Threats to validity of the review method are discussed.
- Full list of included papers is available in an appendix or a link.
