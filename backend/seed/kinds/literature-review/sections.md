# Default sections

1. **Abstract** (200 to 300 words). Why the review, questions, how many papers from
   where, main findings, main gaps.
2. **Introduction** (1 page). The area and why it needs mapping now; research
   questions; contributions of the review.
3. **Background** (0.5 to 1 page). Key terms defined; prior reviews and how this one
   differs.
4. **Method** (1.5 to 2 pages). Protocol followed (for example PRISMA or Kitchenham);
   search strategy with exact strings and databases; inclusion and exclusion
   criteria; screening process with a funnel figure; data extraction form; quality
   assessment; threats to validity of the method.
5. **Classification scheme** (0.5 to 1 page). The taxonomy used to organise findings
   and how it was derived.
6. **Results** (3 to 5 pages). One subsection per research question; tables and
   charts with counts; representative papers cited per category.
7. **Discussion** (1 to 2 pages). Synthesis across questions; trends over time; gaps
   as concrete empty cells in the taxonomy; implications for research and practice.
8. **Threats to validity** (0.5 page). Coverage, selection, extraction, publication
   bias.
9. **Conclusion** (0.5 page). Answers to each question in one or two sentences; the
   most important gap.
10. **Appendix**: full list of included papers, extraction data availability.
