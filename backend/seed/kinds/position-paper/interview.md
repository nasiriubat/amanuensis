# Interview rounds

## Round 1: The claim

- The claim in one sentence. Could a reasonable colleague disagree with it?
- What tension, failure or trend prompted it?
- Who is the audience and what do they currently believe?

## Round 2: Reasons

- List the reasons for the claim. Aim for two to four.
- For each reason: what evidence supports it? Literature, data, experience?
- Which reason is strongest? Which is weakest?

## Round 3: Objections

- The best argument against your claim, stated as its proponent would state it.
- Your reply to it.
- Any other objections worth addressing.
- Is there a version of your claim you would concede?

## Round 4: Implications and agenda

- If people accepted the claim, what would they do differently tomorrow?
- Concrete research questions or actions, numbered.
- Who is positioned to do each?
- What would show the claim was wrong?
