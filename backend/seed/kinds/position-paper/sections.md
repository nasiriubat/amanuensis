# Default sections

Position papers are usually short (4 to 8 pages). Lengths below assume 6 pages.

1. **Abstract** (100 to 200 words). The tension, the claim, the main reason, the call.
2. **Introduction** (0.75 to 1 page). The situation and the tension; the claim in one
   sentence; roadmap of the argument.
3. **Background** (0.5 page, optional). Terms and the current state, only as much as
   the argument needs.
4. **The argument** (2 to 3 pages). One subsection per reason; each with evidence and
   citations; a running example if it helps.
5. **Objections and replies** (0.75 to 1 page). The strongest counterarguments, each
   stated fairly and answered.
6. **Implications** (0.5 to 1 page). What changes for research, practice, teaching or
   policy.
7. **Research agenda** or **Call to action** (0.5 page). Numbered, concrete, with
   who could do what.
8. **Conclusion** (0.25 page). The claim again, in new words.
