# Required evidence

## Introduction

- Claim stated in one sentence that someone could disagree with.
- The tension or trigger described concretely.

## Argument

- Each reason has its own subsection.
- Each reason cites evidence: literature, data or documented experience.
- No reason rests on anecdote alone.

## Objections

- At least two objections stated in the proponent's terms.
- Each objection answered, or conceded in part.

## Implications and agenda

- Implications are concrete actions, not "pay more attention".
- Agenda items are numbered and specific.
- States what evidence would refute the claim.
