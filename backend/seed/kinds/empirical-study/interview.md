# Interview rounds

## Round 1: Question

- What did you want to find out, and why does it matter?
- Research questions or hypotheses as you would write them.
- What did prior work find, and what is still unknown or disputed?

## Round 2: Design

- Study type: experiment, survey, interviews, case study, repository mining, mixed.
- Why this design for this question?
- Independent and dependent variables, or the phenomena of interest.
- Instruments: questionnaires, tasks, interview guides. Were they piloted?

## Round 3: Participants or data

- How many participants, cases, repositories or records?
- Who or what were they, and how were they selected?
- Why is that sample adequate for the claim you want to make?
- Consent, anonymisation and ethics approval.

## Round 4: Analysis and results

- Analysis method: which tests, which coding approach, which tools.
- Per research question, the finding in one sentence with the key number or quote.
- Effect sizes and confidence intervals where they exist.
- Unexpected results.

## Round 5: Meaning and validity

- What do the results mean for researchers? For practitioners?
- Where do they agree or disagree with prior work?
- Threats to validity, one per type, and what was done about each.
- What would the next study be?
- Where is the data or the analysis script available?
