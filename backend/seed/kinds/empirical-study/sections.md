# Default sections

1. **Abstract** (150 to 250 words). Context, question, method with sample size, main
   finding, implication.
2. **Introduction** (1 page). Why the question matters; the gap; research questions;
   contributions.
3. **Background and related work** (1 to 1.5 pages). Concepts; prior studies and
   what they found; what remains open.
4. **Method** (1.5 to 2.5 pages). Study design; participants or data and their
   selection; instruments or measures; procedure; analysis approach; ethics.
5. **Results** (2 to 3 pages). One subsection per research question; tables and
   figures; quotes for qualitative data; no interpretation yet.
6. **Discussion** (1 to 1.5 pages). What the results mean; comparison with prior
   findings; implications for research and practice.
7. **Threats to validity** (0.5 to 0.75 page). Construct, internal, external,
   conclusion validity, and what was done about each.
8. **Conclusion** (0.25 to 0.5 page). Answer per question; next study.
9. **Data availability and ethics statement**.
