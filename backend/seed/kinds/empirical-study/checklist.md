# Required evidence

## Introduction

- Research questions or hypotheses stated before the method.
- Gap in prior work stated with references.

## Method

- Study design named and justified.
- Participant or data count reported with selection procedure.
- Instruments described or made available.
- Analysis approach named (tests, coding method, tools).
- Ethics: consent and approval stated, or explained as not applicable.

## Results

- Each research question answered in its own subsection.
- Quantitative results include effect sizes or confidence intervals, not p-values alone.
- Qualitative results include representative quotes and the coding scheme.
- Results contain no interpretation; interpretation is in the discussion.

## Discussion and validity

- Compares findings with prior studies.
- Threats to validity covered by type with mitigation.
- Generalisation claims are limited to the population studied.

## Whole paper

- Data or analysis scripts available, or a reason given.
