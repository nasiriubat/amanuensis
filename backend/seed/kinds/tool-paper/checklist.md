# Required evidence

Items are seeded into the project checklist. Each must be satisfied in the text or
turned into a stated limitation before export.

## Introduction

- Names the users and their problem with a concrete scenario.
- States contributions as a numbered or bulleted list.
- Every contribution listed is backed by a section later in the paper.

## Related work

- Positions the tool against at least two named alternatives.
- Says what none of the alternatives do, in one paragraph.

## Design

- Has an architecture figure with every component mentioned in the text.
- Gives the reason for each major design decision.
- Uses a running example at least once.

## Evaluation

- States research questions or evaluation goals before results.
- Reports the number of participants or cases and how they were obtained.
- Every quantitative claim has a table, figure or number in the text.
- Reports at least one negative or mixed result, or explains why there was none.
- Has a threats-to-validity paragraph.

## Whole paper

- Tool availability stated: URL, licence, version.
- No claim in the abstract that is absent from the evaluation.
- Limitations appear in the section where they matter, not only at the end.
