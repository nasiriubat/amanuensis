# Interview rounds

Generic rounds. The model adapts them using the learned playbook.

## Round 1: Contribution

- What is the paper about, in two sentences?
- What is new? What would a reader be unable to find elsewhere?
- Who should read it and what should they take away?

## Round 2: Evidence

- What supports the claims? Data, experience, argument, artefacts?
- What are the key numbers, names or facts that must appear?

## Round 3: Context

- Which prior work is closest, and how does this differ?
- Which venue, and what does it expect?

## Round 4: Limits

- What can this paper not claim?
- What is missing that a reviewer would ask for?
