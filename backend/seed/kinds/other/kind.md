# Other

A paper that does not fit the built-in kinds, or one where you want the structure to
come entirely from your exemplars. This kind ships empty on purpose: the outline,
interview and checklist are derived from the playbook learned from exemplar papers.

## When to use it

- Experience reports, dataset papers, vision papers, doctoral symposium papers,
  demos, extended abstracts, or anything else.
- When you have strong exemplars and want them to drive everything.

## When not to use it

- If your paper matches a built-in kind even loosely, pick that kind. You still get
  the exemplar-derived playbook, plus a sensible fallback when exemplars are few.
- If you find yourself using "other" repeatedly for the same shape of paper, ask an
  admin to add a new kind.
