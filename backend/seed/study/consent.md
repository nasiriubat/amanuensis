# Consent to take part in a usability study of Paper Writer

**Study.** We are evaluating Paper Writer, a self-hosted tool that helps researchers write a
paper from what they built or plan to study. Five participants each write one paper with it
over two weeks. The study is run by [study lead, affiliation]. Contact: [email].

**What taking part involves.** You use the tool for one paper of your own choosing, fill in a
questionnaire (about ten minutes) and take part in a recorded interview of about twenty
minutes. Total time beyond your own writing: about half an hour.

**What is recorded.**
- Usage events in the tool: pages opened, sections saved, drafted, fixed or exported, with
  timestamps, and token counts per model call. The tool never records the text you type.
- The paper you produce, its intermediate versions and the tool's outputs (drafts, critiques).
- The questionnaire answers and the interview recording, transcribed and then deleted.

**How the data are used.** To describe where time and effort go, how often participants edit
or reject the tool's output, and what breaks. Results are reported in aggregate or with
pseudonyms. Quotes from the interview are used only with your permission at the time. Your
paper's content is never published by us; only counts and categories derived from it.

**Storage.** Data stay on the university's server for the tool and on the study lead's
encrypted drive for the questionnaire and interview. They are deleted [retention, for example
one year after the study ends] or earlier on request.

**Your rights.** Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason,
and you can ask for your data to be deleted, before or after the study, by writing to the
study lead. Stopping has no consequences for you.
