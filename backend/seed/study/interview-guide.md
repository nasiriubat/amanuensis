# Interview guide (twenty minutes, semi-structured)

Record with permission. Ask the participant to open their project while you talk.

## Opening (2 min)
- Tell me about the paper you wrote. Where did you start: something built, an idea or a draft?

## The path (6 min)
- Walk me through what you did in your first session. What did you expect to happen next, and
  did it?
- Which steps did you skip? Why?
- Show me a moment where you did not know what to do.

## The output (6 min)
- Open a section the tool drafted. What did you keep, what did you rewrite, what did you delete?
- Did the draft ever say something you had not told it? How did you notice?
- How did you handle the placeholders? Did the Search button or the scan help with citations?
- Did you use Fix issues? Did you accept or discard what it proposed, and why?

## Trust and ownership (4 min)
- Whose paper is this? What would make you feel more or less in control?
- Would you put your name on a paper drafted this way? What would have to be true?

## Closing (2 min)
- If you could change one thing, what would it be?
- Anything you expected the tool to do that it did not?

## After the interview
Note within an hour: three themes, two quotes, one bug or improvement request.
