# Participant guide

Thank you for taking part. Over the next two weeks you will write one paper with this tool,
about your own tool, system or planned study. There is no right way to use it. Use it the way
you would if nobody were watching, and tell us where it got in your way.

## What we ask of you

1. **One paper, your own topic.** A tool paper, an empirical study, a position paper or a
   literature review. A draft you already started is fine: choose "I have a draft" when you
   create the project.
2. **Two weeks, at your own pace.** Most people spend three to six hours in total. You do not
   need to finish a submittable paper; a complete first draft with a reviewer pass is enough.
3. **Two short forms and one conversation.** A questionnaire at the end (ten minutes) and a
   twenty-minute interview with the study lead.

## How to start

1. Sign in with the email you received. Change the temporary password when asked.
2. Create a project. Pick the starting point that matches you: "I built something",
   "I have an idea" or "I have a draft".
3. Follow the "Next up" card on the project page. Every page has a footer that says what
   comes next. You can skip any step marked optional.
4. When the Studio has drafted a section, read it as a reviewer would. Edit freely: once you
   change a section it is yours and the tool asks before touching it again.
5. When you have a draft, run Review, then Export a PDF.

## What the tool does and does not do

- It writes only from what you give it: your specification, your answers, your facts.
  Gaps become `[NEEDS: …]` placeholders, not invented sentences.
- It cites only references you have verified. A claim without a source becomes a
  `[CITE: …]` placeholder with a Search button.
- It does not send your text anywhere except to the model provider the administrator chose.

## What we record

With your consent, the tool records which pages you open and when, which sections you save,
draft, fix or export, and the tokens each model call used. It never records what you type.
The study lead sees this per project, alongside the paper itself, to understand where time
and effort go. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any point, including after the study.

## If something goes wrong

Write down what you expected and what happened, with the time. A screenshot helps. Send it to
the study lead or paste it into the side chat of your project with the word "BUG"; we read those.
